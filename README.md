# Bright-Coffee-Shop
Project overview: Bright Coffee Shop
vision ablnd concept:

Mission
- Creating an understandable platform for our Chief executive officer (CEO), data our mission is to make it easy for our stakeholders to work with the shop's data,
to make proper data driven decisions and grow the business and help the new Chief executive officer (CEO) settle in the business.

Target audience 
- Stakeholders and the new CEO

process: 
-Data will be collected from the company's database and ingested into data cleaning/processing platform, then analysed based on the business's performance,to figure out ways for the new CEO to act on.Then after collecting, cleaning and and analysing the data a presentation will be made and presented to our stakeholders and the new CEO, after decisions are made the analysed data will continue to be monitored.

Tools used:
- Excel: Excel will be used to collect the raw data from the company's database. 
- Databricks: Databricks will be the platform used to clean/process our data.
- Power BI: Power BI will be used to analyse the data and give a clear view of the data.
- Power point: A presentation will then be created using power point.
